Perfect matchings in hypergraphs and Feige's inequality

Expository note

arXiv PDF

Feige's inequality gives an upper tail bound for sums of independent random variables, and is useful in many combinatorial contexts. The conjecture was proven last summer, with relatively complicated and not self-contained proofs. Our hope in this expository note was to present a simplified and self-contained proof, presented in a way which motivates the steps of the proof. We also discuss a combinatorial application of this inequality to the problem of perfect matchings in hypergraphs of large minimum degree.
