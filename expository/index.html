Perfect matchings in hypergraphs and Feige's inequality

Expository note · Joint work with Benny Sudakov

arXiv PDF

Feige's inequality gives an upper tail bound for sums of independent nonnegative random variables and has many combinatorial applications. In this expository note, we present a simplified, self-contained proof and explain the motivation behind each step. We also discuss its application to perfect matchings in hypergraphs with large minimum degree.
